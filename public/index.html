📋 What Gets Validated

  • Formatting: Font (12pt/11pt/10pt), margins, 1.5/1.0 spacing, hanging indent, dashes
  • Citations: CMS 17th notes & bibliography, city of publication, page numbers, no Latin terms
  • Structure: Decimal headings (1., 1.1, 1.1.1), cover page, declaration, organization
  • Quotations: Footnote placement after punctuation, block quotes (>4 lines), quote marks
  • Biblical & SBL Style: Parenthetical Bible abbreviations, BCE/CE, AWA
